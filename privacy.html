Privacy

Nothing about you reaches a server.

VibeFounder's site and its bench run in your browser. There is no account and no analytics.

What the bench keeps

  • Your answers to the bench (your craft, the domain, the hours, the name you type) live in this tab's session storage. They go when you close the tab.
  • The first week's checklist and the language you chose live in this browser's local storage, on your device. Clear the site's data to remove them.
  • The founding agreement is drawn in this browser. It is copied or downloaded only when you press the button.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookie and loads no third-party script, font, image or tracker. Every file comes from this site.
  • It sends nothing: the page's policy sets connect-src 'none'.
  • It asks for no account, no email address and no payment.

Leaving the site

The ecosystem list links to the other vibe startups' own sites. Once you follow one, that site's privacy notice applies.

Writing to us

If you send an email to hello@vibefounder.dev, we keep it to answer you.
