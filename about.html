VibeFounder

The founder-facing hub of the vibe ecosystem. Build, fund, ship, govern.

What it is

VibeFounder is where a founder starts. It does two things: it matches a founder with a co-founder who covers the gap the team has, and it points the founder at the vibe startups that make the tools, the money, the operations and the filings a young company needs.

It is one startup of CommandAGI's vibe family, not a page of CommandAGI. Its operating home is companies/vibefounder/ in CommandAGI's Drive; this site is its own repository, CommandAGI/site-vibefounder, and CommandAGI is its vendor, not its product.

What the bench does with your answers

Nothing leaves this browser. The bench reads the craft, the domain and the hours you choose, ranks ten fictional sample co-founders against them, and draws the founding agreement and the first week's checklist. The answers live in this tab's session storage; the checklist lives in your browser's local storage. There is no account, no server call and no cookie. The page's own policy forbids a connection (connect-src 'none').

What the sample co-founders are not

They are not people. Every one is fictional, marked "Sample co-founder", and drawn to show the matching. VibeFounder matches no real founder with a real co-founder yet; when it does, a person will read each pair, and the terms will be a lawyer's, not a draft.

The family

The vibe startups are each their own company with their own site: VibeStartup (the startup factory), VibeCAD, VibeCode, VibeCloud, VibeCut, VibeForce, VibeFund and VibeGov. The list, with links, is on the home page.
