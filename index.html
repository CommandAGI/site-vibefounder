The bench runs in this browser on fictional sample co-founders. It sends nothing, asks for no account, and costs nothing.

What the bench does

Three things a founder needs on day one: a partner, a brief, a map.

It matches the gap

Tell the bench what you are building, your craft and the hours you can give. It ranks the sample co-founders by what your team is missing, and says why each one fits: the craft you lack, the domain you share, the commitment that matches.

It drafts the founding agreement

Pick a co-founder and the bench writes a founding agreement you can read in full: equal founders, four years of vesting with a one-year cliff, the work assigned to the company, and the decisions that need both of you. Keep it as a file and take it to a lawyer.

It draws the map

The vibe ecosystem in one place: the startups that make the tools a young company uses, and the ones that fund it, govern it and ship it. Each is its own company with its own site.
